In September 2026, d-Matrix and Nvidia announced a multi-year collaboration to bring d-Matrix inference accelerators into Nvidia’s MGX rack-scale architecture through NVLink Fusion. Two months earlier, Parasail began deploying d-Matrix Corsair accelerators alongside its Nvidia Hopper and Blackwell fleet in production. Neither of those is a story about an accelerator beating a GPU.
